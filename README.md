## 🕹️ About

Online: 404 is an open-world survival simulator set in the abandoned, quarantined Leminsgale District. Survive solo or with friends by scavenging, crafting, hunting, farming, fortifying shelters, restoring vehicles, and uncovering the truth behind the district’s collapse. Join the beta testing program, help shape development, and receive a free game key.

Online: 404 is built for players who enjoy story-driven survival, open-world exploration, resource management, base building, and cooperative adventures.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- 📱 **Smartphone Survival** — Use your in-game smartphone for maps, weather, research, crafting information, and more.
- 🌲 **Open-World Exploration** — Explore the abandoned Leminsgale District, discover points of interest, and uncover hidden resources.
- 🏠 **Shelter Building** — Construct, reinforce, and customize a shelter using practical materials found throughout the world.
- 🧰 **Realistic Crafting** — Use scavenged equipment and available knowledge to create tools, supplies, and survival solutions.
- 🧟 **Hostile Survivors** — Face dangerous former residents and defend your shelter against raids and unexpected threats.
- 🐾 **Living Wilderness** — Observe animals, hunt for food, farm resources, and discover the dangers of the surrounding environment.
- 🚗 **Vehicle Restoration** — Repair an abandoned car to travel more efficiently across the district.
- 🤝 **Solo and Co-op Play** — Experience the story alone or team up with friends for cooperative survival.
